🔬 Inferential Hypothesis Testing (Independent Two-Sample Welch t-test)

Testing difference in stress score between Short Sleep (< 6 hrs) and Adequate Sleep (≥ 6 hrs) cohorts.

Group 1 (Sleep < 6h, n = 125): Mean Stress = 58.14 Group 2 (Sleep ≥ 6h, n = 75): Mean Stress = 25.19 Welch Two Sample t-test: t-statistic = 15.344, df = 156.27, p-value = 2.6876e-33 (< 0.001) Conclusion: Reject H0. Severe sleep deficit significantly increases student stress by an average of 32.95 points.
